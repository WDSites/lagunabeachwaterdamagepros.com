Water spreading under the floor: what slab leak cleanup means

Slab leak water damage cleanup in Laguna Beach, CA is the drying and removal work that follows a water line failing inside or beneath a concrete slab floor. The pipe repair belongs to a plumber. Our part is everything the water touched: carpet, padding, wood or laminate flooring, baseboards, the bottom of the drywall and the surface of the slab itself.

Slab water moves slowly and quietly. It can spread under flooring for days before anyone sees a stain, so by the time a leak is found the damage is often wider than it looks.

A narrower problem on Laguna Beach hillsides

Slab leaks only happen in homes built on a concrete slab. The City of Laguna Beach Housing Element says development here is shaped by steep hillside terrain, and many homes on those slopes sit on raised foundations or have lower levels cut into the hill. That makes a true slab leak a narrower case here than on a flat inland lot.

The same document counts 13,027 housing units in 2020, with 71.3 percent single-unit homes and a good share of condos and apartments. If your floor is concrete, this page is for you. If your house sits on posts and a crawl space, read about crawl-space water damage instead, because the water behaves very differently down there.

Signs the water is coming from under the slab

Homeowners usually notice a symptom long before they hear the word slab. The signs below each point to a different part of the story, so it helps to know what they mean.

Warm spots on the floor

A patch of floor that feels warm underfoot often means a hot water line is leaking beneath it. The heated water warms the concrete directly above the pipe. Walk the room barefoot and mark any spot that stays warm.

A meter that runs with everything off

Shut off every tap, appliance and irrigation line, then look at the water meter. If the dial or low-flow indicator still moves, water is escaping somewhere. The Laguna Beach County Water District supplies most of the city, and the City of Laguna Beach water-quality page points South Laguna residents to the South Coast Water District, so call whichever one bills you if you need meter help.

Damp baseboards, wet carpet and hairline cracks

Baseboards that feel cool and damp, carpet that squelches in one zone, or a hairline crack with a wet edge all suggest moisture rising through the slab. A water bill that climbs with no change in habits is the quietest clue of all. Any one of these is enough reason to call.

First-hour safety

Turn off the main water valve to stop the supply. If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, leave the power off in that area and stay out of any wet zone. Ready.gov says not to touch electrical equipment if it is wet or if you are standing in water. Call a licensed electrician if the breaker box is wet.

First-hour safety: who does what

Shut off the main, lift small items off the floor and photograph the wet areas before anything moves. Keep children and pets out of the wet room. Then make two calls: a plumber for leak detection and our 24/7 line for the water damage.

We are not plumbers, so we do not locate or repair the pipe. The plumber will test the lines, pinpoint the leak and often break through the slab to reach it. We do not cut concrete for plumbing and we do not do electrical work. We stop the spread of moisture and dry what the water reached.

How we dry a slab leak loss

The crew follows a set order so the plumber and the dryers never block each other. Ask any company whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published industry standard for water damage restoration.

  1. 1

    Map the moisture

    We scan with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera to find where water traveled under flooring and up the walls. The readings become a map, and the map sets where equipment goes.

  2. 2

    Remove what cannot dry

    Soaked carpet padding, swollen baseboard and saturated drywall at the base are cut out and bagged. Standing water goes into an extraction unit first.

  3. 3

    Dry the structure

    Air movers push air across the slab surface and wall bases while LGR dehumidifiers pull moisture out of the room. Injectable drying can help when water sits behind trim or in cavities.

  4. 4

    Check and document

    We re-read the meters each visit and only pull equipment once the readings come back to normal. Your plumber's repair may happen midway, and we plan around it.

Why flooring and slab surfaces get different treatment

Concrete is porous, so a wet slab holds moisture for a long time and releases it upward into whatever covers it. Engineered wood, laminate and glued-down floors are hit hardest because they trap that vapor against the slab. Carpet is often the easiest to save if the water was clean and the response was quick, and carpet water damage explains when it can stay.

The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas and items within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth. A slab that stays damp beneath new flooring defeats that window, which is why we measure the slab itself before anyone lays anything back down.

What changes the cost of a slab leak cleanup

We explain scope and price before work starts. These are the factors that move the number, and we do not quote amounts on a web page.

How far the water spread

One room costs less to dry than a hallway, a kitchen and two bedrooms. The moisture map sets the real footprint.

Flooring type

Carpet can often be dried or replaced. Glued wood and laminate usually come up, and that adds removal work.

Water category

A clean supply line is category 1. If the water sat or picked up contamination, the cleanup steps and the materials we discard change.

Drying days and equipment

More air movers and dehumidifiers running more days costs more. A dense slab can take longer than drywall.

Rebuild scope

Replacing baseboards, drywall and flooring after drying is a separate scope from the drying itself.

Insurance and slab leaks

Coverage for plumbing leaks often depends on whether the damage was sudden and accidental or a slow leak that went on for months. Gradual damage and neglect are commonly excluded, so check your policy and ask your insurer how it treats leaks under a slab, including the cost of breaking into the concrete.

The California Department of Insurance residential claims guide tells homeowners to make temporary repairs to prevent further damage, since policies may not cover ensuing damage if reasonable steps were skipped. It also says to keep a log of calls and copies of correspondence. Its flood guidance adds that flood policies typically exclude water from burst pipes inside the home.

Mistakes to avoid after a slab leak

Do not lay new flooring over a slab that has not been measured dry. Do not run a space heater to speed things up, because heat can push moisture deeper and into wall bases. Do not ignore a small meter movement just because nothing looks wet yet.

Also avoid hiring anyone for restoration or repair without checking the license. The Contractors State License Board lists a C-61/D64 water damage remediation classification on a license record, and you can check any contractor's license on the CSLB site. The permit question for floor and drywall repair belongs with the City of Laguna Beach Community Development Department.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is emergency slab leak water damage cleanup available in Laguna Beach, CA?

Yes. Our dispatch line runs 24 hours a day and we send a crew the same day when available. We make no promise about arrival time, but a call at night starts the same process as a call at noon. Shut off the main valve first if you can reach it.

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Laguna Beach?

Yes. Dispatch is open around the clock, including weekends and holidays. Tell the dispatcher what you found, whether the main is off and which rooms are wet, and the crew plans equipment before arriving.

Do you repair the leaking pipe?

No. We are not plumbers. A plumber does leak detection and the repair, which often means breaking into the slab. We dry the flooring, carpet, baseboards and slab surface and remove what cannot be saved, and we can work around the plumber's schedule.

My home is on a hillside. Can I still have a slab leak?

Only if part of the floor is a concrete slab. Many hillside homes here sit on raised foundations or have lower levels, which behave differently. If you have a crawl space, see crawl-space water damage.

What does slab leak cleanup cost?

It depends on how far the water spread, the flooring type, the water category, how many days of drying are needed and what has to be rebuilt. We explain scope and price before work starts. Want your real number? Call.

Will my insurance cover a slab leak?

Sudden and accidental leaks are often covered, while slow leaks and neglect often are not. Coverage for breaking the slab varies by policy. Check your policy and ask your insurer, and keep photos and a log of every call.
