Aliso Viejo is a newer town, and its homes leak in newer ways

The city's Housing Element says over two-thirds of Aliso Viejo's housing stock was built after 1990. Newer construction usually means fewer old corroded lines and more appliance hookups, plastic fittings, braided supply hoses and water heaters as the usual culprits. When you call, tell us what failed, because the source decides how we dry the structure.

The mix matters as much as the age. The same document counts about 35% single-family detached, 26% attached townhouses and 39% multi-family. That means roughly 65% of homes share a wall, floor or ceiling with a neighbor.

The 2020 Census counted 52,176 people on 6.93 square miles. In a layout that tight, a failed dishwasher line often has a neighbor's ceiling on the other side, so say which unit and floor when you call.

Attached and multi-story homes: how a leak travels between units

Water follows gravity and framing. It runs down wall studs, along floor joists, through gaps around pipe penetrations and into the ceiling cavity below. An overflowing upstairs tub can show up as a brown stain two units away, long after the damage started.

A moisture meter and a thermal imaging camera let a crew trace that path without tearing out every wall. We map which rooms read wet, which materials hold water, and where the wet area stops. In a townhouse or condo, that map often crosses a property line.

HOA and association responsibilities: who handles what

The city's Housing Element includes a policy on coordinating with homeowner associations, and much of Aliso Viejo's attached housing sits under one. Where your responsibility ends and the association's begins is set by your governing documents. In many associations the building shell is the association's side and the interior is yours, but it varies, so read yours.

If the water came from a shared pipe, a roof or another unit, call your management company at once and take photos before anything is moved. Keep a log of every call and email, as the California Department of Insurance advises for claims.

If you rent, tell your landlord the same day, and we can start drying while that conversation continues.

When water is spreading in Aliso Viejo: what we send first

Emergency dispatch is open around the clock, nights and weekends included, and we offer same-day response when available. The order of work matters in a town of shared walls, so repair and mitigation lead.

Water mitigation comes first. The crew extracts standing water, then places air movers and LGR dehumidifiers and checks progress with a moisture meter each day. The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas and items within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, which is why speed matters.

Once the structure reads dry, water damage repair rebuilds what was removed: drywall, baseboards, insulation, flooring and paint. In a condo that often means matching finishes to the neighboring rooms.

If the water was dirty, water damage cleanup covers sanitizing and removing affected materials. For a burst line that has flooded a floor, emergency water removal pulls the water out before it soaks into carpet pad and cabinet bases.

If water is still spreading, do this before the crew arrives

These steps fit a townhouse, condo or detached home. Do only what is safe.

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve, or the main valve for your home. In a condo you may need the association or building management to close a shared line, so call them right away if you cannot find the shutoff.

Power down wet areas only if safe

If water is dripping through a ceiling light or you are standing in water, do not touch switches or outlets. Ready.gov says not to touch electrical equipment if it is wet or if you are standing in water.

Lift what you can

Move electronics, books and rugs away from the wet zone, especially under a stained ceiling. Put foil or blocks under furniture legs on wet carpet.

Keep people and pets out

Water from a washer drain, toilet or sewer line can carry contamination. Keep children and pets out of any water you cannot trace to a clean supply line.

Call us, then knock next door

Call the 24/7 line, then tell the neighbors above, below or beside you. Take photos of every wet surface for your log.

Water district, permits and flood maps in Aliso Viejo

Moulton Niguel Water District supplies drinking water, recycled water and wastewater service across Aliso Viejo and five other cities. If a sewer line is backing up, ask the district whether the blockage sits in your lateral or in its main, because that decides who pays for the fix. We handle the cleanup and drying, not the pipe.

The city's own Building Department handles code enforcement from City Hall at 12 Journey, Suite 100. We cannot say whether a specific repair needs a permit, so ask the City of Aliso Viejo before you close up walls.

The city code adopts the Orange County Flood Insurance Study of February 18, 2004 and its flood maps. Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address, since flood is usually excluded from standard homeowners policies. Sudden and accidental leaks are often covered, so check your policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Aliso Viejo?

We cover 92656, the ZIP code for the town. It includes City Hall at 12 Journey, Soka University of America at 1 University Drive and the residential streets around them.

Aliso Viejo also has a corporate-only ZIP that has no homes, so there is nothing to dry there. If you are not sure your address is in town, give dispatch the street and we will confirm.

Is emergency water removal available in Aliso Viejo?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7, including nights and weekends, with same-day response when available. Call, describe what failed, and the crew that arrives will extract the water and start drying.

See our emergency water removal service for what that looks like on site.

My upstairs neighbor's leak is coming through my ceiling. What do I do?

Call us and your association's management at the same time. Take photos of the ceiling, walls and floor, and keep a log of every call. Do not stand under a sagging ceiling, since saturated drywall can drop.

Is water damage in my condo covered by the HOA or my insurance?

It depends on where the water came from and what your governing documents say. Check your policy and ask your insurer. Sudden and accidental damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not.

Do I need a permit for water damage repair in Aliso Viejo?

We cannot tell you for your address. Ask the City of Aliso Viejo Building Department before you rebuild walls, especially if plumbing or electrical work is involved.
