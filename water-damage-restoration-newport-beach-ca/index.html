Rain season and high tides in Newport Beach

Water damage restoration in Newport Beach, CA is a different job from the same work inland, because the harbor, the ocean and the rain all push on the same low ground. Our crews drive in from Laguna Beach, about ten miles up Pacific Coast Highway, and dispatch stays open 24/7 through the wet months.

The City of Newport Beach describes its flood hazard areas as subject to periodic inundation, and its floodplain page lists a FEMA flood map effective March 21, 2019. We cannot tell you which zone your house sits in. Check the FEMA flood map for your address, or ask the city Building Division, before you decide what your policy might cover.

Bayfront and island buildings: Balboa Island, the Peninsula and the harbor islands

Balboa Island was dredged and filled between 1905 and 1913, and its first bulkheads were built to hold back high tides. A city seawall document gives the island seawall crest as 7.6 to 8.7 feet, and a 2017 city contract raised about 7,400 linear feet of cap by nine inches. If your ground floor sits near street level, photograph the waterline before anything is moved.

The city lists the Balboa Peninsula as a three-mile strip between the harbor and the ocean, and names Lido, Collins, Linda and Newport Island among the harbor islands. A 2019 report described Balboa Island streets flooding in hard rain, with tide gates opened by hand. Harbor and street water is Category 3 until proven otherwise, so keep people and pets out and expect carpet pad to come out.

Who supplies your water: check your bill before you shut off the meter

Water service in Newport Beach is split. The City of Newport Beach Utilities Department serves part of town, Irvine Ranch Water District serves the southern half including all of Newport Coast, and Mesa Water also appears in the city's water-district map. Exact boundaries were not published in a form we could confirm.

That matters at 2 a.m. with a failed supply line. Your water bill names the agency, and that agency is who you call about a meter shutoff or a street-side leak. Your own main valve inside the house is still the fastest fix, and we can talk you through finding it.

When water is spreading in Newport Beach

Call the 24/7 line the moment water is moving across a floor or dripping through a ceiling. We send licensed and insured restoration professionals, and same-day response is available when crews are free. We do not promise an arrival time, but we will tell you honestly what to do while the crew is on the way.

Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras find water hiding inside walls. The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth.

If water is still spreading in your Newport Beach home

Do these in order while you wait for the crew.

Stop the source

Close the main valve inside the house or at the meter. In a condo or a harbor-side townhome, find the building shutoff, since a neighbor's unit above may be the source.

Power down wet areas only if safe

Switch off breakers for wet rooms only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Ready.gov says not to touch electrical equipment if it is wet or if you are standing in water.

Lift what you can

Move small furniture, rugs, electronics and documents onto counters or to upper floors. On Balboa Island and the Peninsula, garage and ground-level storage is the first to get wet.

Keep out of contaminated water

Tidewater, storm runoff and sewage backups can carry bacteria. Keep children, pets and yourself out of it, and do not walk or drive through flood water.

Call and document

Call the 24/7 line, then take photos and video of every wet surface before cleanup. Keep a log of calls with your insurer.

Services we send first in Newport Beach

Most calls here begin with emergency water removal: extraction units pull standing water from floors before it wicks into drywall and cabinets. When tides or storm surf put water inside a home, flood damage restoration covers the contaminated-water side, including removal of porous materials and sanitizing of what stays.

After the water is out, water mitigation sets up the drying: air movers, LGR dehumidifiers and daily moisture readings until the materials reach their dry standard. Then water damage cleanup handles debris, odor and surface treatment so the rebuild can begin. If drying was delayed, mold remediation is available, and mold needs moisture to grow, which is why speed matters.

Permits, flood maps and insurance in Newport Beach

The city runs a Coastal Flood Recovery Program with same-day online Express Permit service for nonstructural repairs in single-family homes. It applies only to areas named in the city's fall and winter 2026 to 2027 emergency proclamations, so ask the Building Division whether your address qualifies before planning repairs.

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, and gradual leaks often are not. Flood is usually excluded from standard homeowners policies and needs separate flood coverage, per the California Department of Insurance. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and note that a flood policy typically takes effect 30 days after you sign.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Newport Beach?

Yes for the four standard Newport Beach ZIPs. 92660 holds City Hall on Civic Center Drive, and 92662 carries the Balboa and Balboa Island names. 92661 and 92663 cover the rest of the Newport Beach-named areas.

Newport Coast has its own preferred city name under ZIP 92657. Call and give your street address, and the dispatcher will tell you whether we can reach you.

Is emergency water removal available in Newport Beach?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, with same-day response when available. We send crews from Laguna Beach, about ten miles away, and do not promise a set arrival time.

My Balboa Island street flooded at high tide. Is that water safe to clean up myself?

Treat it as contaminated. Tidewater and street runoff can carry bacteria and silt, and flood water can hide electrical hazards.

Stay out of it, shut off power only if you can do so safely, and call the 24/7 line before you remove anything.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage in Newport Beach?

Sudden and accidental damage such as a burst pipe is often covered, and slow leaks often are not. Flood from tides or storm surge is usually excluded and needs a separate flood policy.

Check your policy or ask your insurer, and photograph damage before cleanup.
