Guides for Laguna Beach homeowners

These guides answer the questions people ask after a leak, a flood or a musty smell. Each one explains what is happening in plain words, what you can do in the first hours, and when to call a professional. They use public sources such as the EPA, Ready.gov and the California Department of Insurance, and they name the source in the text.

The guides are information only. They are not a substitute for an on-site assessment, a plumber's diagnosis or advice from your insurer. Every house is different, and the right fix depends on what a crew finds with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera.

If water is actively spreading in your home, skip the reading and call the 24/7 line. The dispatcher can tell you what to do while a crew is on the way. You can also see our services to understand which one fits your situation.

For mold questions, remember that mold needs moisture. Fixing the water problem is part of any answer, which is why our mold remediation page and our water pages work together. If you are in a nearby town, check the service area page too.
