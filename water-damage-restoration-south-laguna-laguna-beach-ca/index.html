A different utility from the rest of town

Most of Laguna Beach gets water from Laguna Beach County Water District (LBCWD). The fact statements about LBCWD cover most of the city. The city's own water-quality page says that if you live in South Laguna, your water comes from South Coast Water District (SCWD).

So for a street valve or meter question, call SCWD, not the district serving downtown. Your own main shutoff is still the fastest fix.

Where South Laguna sits

South Laguna starts just south of Cardinal Drive on Pacific Coast Highway and runs about 2.5 miles to the Dana Point city limit. Compare that side on the Dana Point, CA page.

The strip is narrow, with coast on one side and hillside on the other, so runoff has a short path into garages and lower levels. Check low rooms first after rain. For the whole city, see water damage restoration in Laguna Beach, CA.

What to do first while water is still spreading

Do these while you wait. Skip anything unsafe.

Close your own main valve

Turn off the water at the house first. Then call SCWD if the leak is on the street side of your meter.

Cut power to wet rooms

Only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Ready.gov says not to touch electrical equipment while wet.

Lift what you can

Raise rugs, electronics and boxes off the floor. Leave heavy furniture where it is.

Keep people out of dirty water

Sewage or storm water stays off limits for children and pets. Do not walk through standing water.

Tell us about the gate

If you live in a gated enclave, give the guard or board contact our dispatcher needs so the crew is expected at the entrance.

Gated enclaves and access

South Laguna contains Three Arch Bay and Lagunita, both private gated communities. The community realtor guide describes Three Arch Bay as guarded 24/7, so a night call usually means a gate list, not an open street. Give us the entry contact when you call and we can plan the crew and truck loading around it.

Three Arch Bay has its own Community Services District, which handles security and stormwater for roughly 1,004 residents. South Coast Water District provides water and wastewater there. Stormwater complaints go to the district, and water or sewer problems go to SCWD.

Storm water and the sinkhole of March 2023

Patch reported on March 15, 2023 that storms opened a sinkhole in a South Laguna neighborhood. It is not about one house, but it shows ground and drainage can fail here in heavy rain.

After a storm, check for wet baseboards, soft drywall and water at garage thresholds. Treat runoff as contaminated until a crew checks it. The storm water damage and flood damage restoration pages explain the process.

Drying a South Laguna home

The crew uses moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera to find water behind walls, then extraction units, air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. The EPA says to dry water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth.

Ask any company whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500. See emergency water removal and sewage backup cleanup for each path.

Documentation and your policy

Photograph everything before moving it and keep receipts. The California Department of Insurance says to make temporary repairs and keep a log of calls. Flood is not usually covered by standard homeowners insurance, so check your policy or ask your insurer.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address.

Sewage and storm water

Do not walk, swim or drive through flood waters, which can be contaminated. The City of Laguna Beach says private sewer lateral maintenance is the property owner's responsibility. Call a licensed plumber for the pipe and us for cleanup and drying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which water district do I call in South Laguna?

The City of Laguna Beach says South Laguna water comes from South Coast Water District, not Laguna Beach County Water District. Call SCWD for street-side or meter problems and shut off your own main valve first.

Is emergency water removal available in South Laguna at night?

Yes. Dispatch runs around the clock, with same-day response when available. If you live behind a gate, give us the guard or board contact so the crew can be let in.

Who handles stormwater in Three Arch Bay?

The Three Arch Bay Community Services District provides stormwater and security services. Water and wastewater come from South Coast Water District.

Is there a city contact for South Laguna water and sewer?

The city has a South Laguna Water Sewer Advisory Committee that acts as a liaison to South Coast Water District. It is not an emergency line.
